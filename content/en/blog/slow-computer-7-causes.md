# Slow computer: 7 causes and when to take it in for repair

The computer takes five minutes to start, the browser freezes and the fan sounds like a plane taking off. Before you buy a new one, it's worth finding out **why it's slow**. Often there's a single cause and it's quick to fix. At SmartMenuKJ we look after computers and printers for homes and offices in Kardzhali. Here are the seven causes we see most often, what you can check yourself and when it's time for a repair.

## 1. An old hard drive (HDD)
The number one cause on older PCs and laptops. A mechanical hard drive reads and writes far more slowly than a modern **SSD**. Signs: slow start-up, programs slow to open, the drive light on all the time.

**Fix:** an SSD upgrade. On older machines it's often the change you notice most.

## 2. Not enough memory (RAM)
When memory runs out, the computer uses the disk as temporary memory, which is much slower. Signs: slowdowns with many browser tabs or several programs open.

**Fix:** a memory upgrade, if the computer allows it. We check what can be added.

## 3. Viruses and malware
Viruses and malware run in the background, use the processor and the internet, and can show ads or redirect your browser. Signs: odd pop-ups, new browser toolbars, programs you didn't install.

**Fix:** virus and malware removal. Don't delete things at random: if you suspect a virus, back up your important files first.

## 4. Too many startup programs
Over the years, programs pile up that start with the computer: printer helpers, updaters, chat apps. Each takes a little; together they slow down start-up.

**Do it yourself:** in Windows open Task Manager (Ctrl + Shift + Esc) → Startup and disable what you don't need. If you're not sure what something is, leave it and ask.

## 5. A full disk
When the disk is almost full, the system has no room for temporary files and updates. Signs: low-space warnings, slow updates.

**Do it yourself:** clear Downloads, empty the recycle bin, move photos and videos to an external drive or the cloud. **Back up** what matters before deleting anything.

## 6. Dust and overheating
Dust clogs fans and heatsinks. The processor heats up and slows itself down to avoid overheating. Signs: a loud fan, a hot laptop, slowdowns after a few minutes of use.

**Fix:** an internal clean. On laptops this means taking it apart, so don't try it with a vacuum through the vents.

## 7. An outdated or damaged system
Missing updates, old drivers or corrupted system files also slow the computer down and cause errors. Sometimes a clean install is the quickest route, but only after the data is safe.

## When to take it in
Bring it to a specialist if:
- **it won't start** or restarts by itself;
- **you suspect a virus** and have important files;
- **the drive clicks** or files disappear: stop using it so you don't lose data;
- the laptop **overheats** and needs opening up;
- you want an **SSD and memory upgrade** but don't know what fits;
- you've tried the above and it's still slow.
