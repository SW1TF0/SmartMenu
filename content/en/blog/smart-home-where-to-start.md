# Smart home: where to start

"Smart home" sounds like a big renovation, but it doesn't have to be. You can start with a few bulbs and add bit by bit, or plan the whole house in one go. At SmartMenuKJ we install and set up smart devices in homes, offices, shops and restaurants in Kardzhali and the region. Here is the easiest order to start in.

## What a smart home actually is
A smart home is a set of devices (bulbs, switches, plugs, radiator valves, sensors, locks) connected in **one app on your phone**. You control them from anywhere, and they also run on their own, on a schedule or as part of a scene. At the centre is the **smart home hub**, which links the devices to each other and to the app.

## Step 1: Lighting
Lighting is the easiest start.
- **Smart bulb**: colour and brightness from your phone.
- **Smart relay for a switch**: fits behind your normal wall switch and runs the light on a schedule, while the switch still works.

You'll notice the difference the first evening: the lights come on when you get home and go off when you go to bed.

## Step 2: Plugs on a schedule
A **smart plug** switches whatever is plugged into it on and off on a schedule, such as a lamp or a small appliance. Handy when you want something off while you're away.

## Step 3: Heating room by room
With **smart radiator valves** every room gets its own schedule: the bedroom is warm in the evening, the living room in the afternoon, and rooms you don't use aren't heated for nothing. Warm at home, lower bills.

## Step 4: Sensors and security
A **door and window sensor** sends an alert when something opens. Linked to the lights, it can switch on the hallway lamp; linked to a **siren** or to [security cameras](/cctv-kardzhali.html), it turns the home into a security system. For the front door there are **access keypads** and **smart locks**.

## Step 5: Scenes
Once the devices are in one system, scenes come next:
- **Morning**: lights and heating come on before the alarm.
- **Night**: everything unnecessary goes off with one tap.
- **Holiday**: heating at minimum while sensors and cameras keep an eye on the house.

A smart home can also work with **voice assistants**, and a **robot vacuum** can clean while you're out.

## Smart automation for business
The same ideas work in offices, shops, restaurants and hotels: lighting and heating on a schedule, access for staff only and power off after hours. There is also **energy monitoring**, so you can see where the electricity goes. All from one app.

## DIY or professional installation?
Some devices, like a bulb or a plug, are easy to fit yourself. Relays behind switches, radiator valves and locks are better installed by someone who does it every day, and everything needs to be set up to work together. Our own team does the installation, schedules and scenes, shows you how it works on site and stays with you afterwards.

3. Add a **schedule**, then a **scene**.
