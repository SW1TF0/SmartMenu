# A website for a restaurant or small business: what it needs

Most people search on Google first and only then decide whether to call, book or come in. If your website doesn't answer their questions quickly, they move on to the next result. At SmartMenuKJ we build websites for companies, shops, offices and restaurants in Kardzhali and all over Bulgaria. This is what we think every small business site needs before anything else.

## 1. The answers on the first screen
Visitors should understand three things within seconds: **what you do, where you are and how to reach you**. So the first screen needs:
- a clear headline with the service and the town, e.g. "Café in central Kardzhali";
- **a phone number that dials with one tap**;
- a button for the main action: "Book a table", "Get a quote" or "See the menu".

A beautiful photo is nice, but it doesn't replace these three.

## 2. Phone first, desktop second
Many visitors open your site on a phone, often on the move. That's why we build every site **mobile-first**: text is readable without zooming, buttons are easy to tap and pages **load fast**. A slow site loses customers before they've seen what you offer.

## 3. Address, map and opening hours
Obvious, yet often missing or out of date: the exact address with a map link, opening hours including weekends, and how to get there or where to park if it matters. When your hours change, update both the site and your Google profile.

## 4. Menu, prices or list of services
For a restaurant, the **menu** is what people look for most. It should be text on the page, not a photo of the paper menu that nobody can read on a phone. If prices change often, link the site to a [QR menu](/qr-menu-kardzhali.html) so guests always see the current menu and you change prices in seconds. More in [QR menus: how they work](/blog/qr-menu-how-it-works.html). For a shop or an office, the same applies: a short, clear list of what you offer.

## 5. Bookings and a contact form
Not everyone wants to call. An **online booking or enquiry form** brings in customers outside opening hours too. One of our projects is a booking website for a local business: a fast mobile-first site with an online booking form and automatic confirmation emails.

## 6. Your own domain and email
An address like **yourname.com** with an email on the same domain looks more serious than a free page or a personal email. The domain is yours and stays yours, even if you redesign years later.

## 7. SEO basics so Google can find you
The site has to be built so Google understands it: a separate title and description for each page with the service and the town, clear headings, fast loading, a proper mobile layout and a sitemap so Google finds every page. These basics are built into every site we make.

## 8. Your own look, not a template
Your website is part of your brand, like your sign and your business card. We do **custom design and branding**, not a template visitors have seen ten times elsewhere. Selling handmade items or need boxes and holders made to measure? See [what 3D printing can make](/blog/3d-printing-what-can-be-made.html).

## 9. Support after launch
A website is never finished for good: prices, menus, photos and hours change. Hosting and **support after launch** are part of our work, and you can always contact us directly for a change.

## Quick checklist
- Does the first screen say what you do and where?
- Does the phone number dial with one tap?
- Are the address, map and opening hours current?
- Is the menu or service list text, not a photo?
- Can customers book or write through a form?
- Is the site fast on a phone?
- Do you have your own domain and email?
