CCTV for home and business: what to know before installation

Cameras are only useful if they see the right places, alert you at the right moments and keep footage where you can find it. That's why a few things are worth deciding before anyone drills a hole. At SmartMenuKJ we install camera systems for shops, offices, restaurants, warehouses and homes in Kardzhali and the region. These are the questions we go through with every client.

1. What exactly do you want to see?

Start from the goal, not the number of cameras:

  • Which entrances matter: the front door, the back door, the garage, the car park?
  • Which areas inside: the till, the dining room, the stockroom, the hallway?
  • When do you need it: only after hours, or all the time?

The answers decide how many cameras you need and which type. Entrances and car parks get outdoor cameras; rooms get indoor ones.

2. Home or business: different needs

For a business (shop, office, restaurant, warehouse) the usual priorities are seeing the till, the floor and the stockroom from your phone, and getting an alert when something moves after hours. This is also where access control comes in: a keypad or a card-and-PIN reader so doors open for staff only.

For a home, peace of mind and privacy come first. Indoor and outdoor cameras send an alert to your phone when they detect motion, and we set them up to notify you about people, not leaves. Recording can stay private and local.

3. Phone viewing and alerts

Modern IP cameras can be watched live in a phone app, from anywhere, and motion detection sends you an alert when something happens. Decide in advance who in the family or team should get the alerts.

4. Picture quality and night vision

HD / 4K IP cameras give a clear picture. Most incidents happen in the evening or at night, so night vision is a must for outdoor cameras and unlit areas.

5. Where the footage is stored

Footage can be stored locally, on a device on site, or in the cloud. The choice depends on the property and on how far back you want to be able to go. One of our projects, cameras for a shop, is a four-camera IP system with phone alerts and two weeks of local recording.

6. Power and internet on site

Not every spot has power by the fence or the shed. For places like that there is a solar 4G camera that works without a mains cable. If you need a camera somewhere remote, mention it at the start.

7. Sensors and a siren

A camera shows you what happens; a door and window sensor tells you the moment something opens, and an alarm siren goes off after hours. Together they make a security system, not just a recording.

8. Who installs and who maintains it

Good equipment can still work badly if it's installed in a hurry. We plan the job, install and set everything up on site with our own team, and show you how to use it. Support after installation is part of the job.

Quick checklist

  • Which entrances and rooms need to be covered?
  • Indoor, outdoor or both?
  • Who will watch on the phone and get the alerts?
  • Local or cloud recording?
  • Any spots without power (solar 4G camera)?
  • Do you need access control, sensors or a siren?
