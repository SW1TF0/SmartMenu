# QR menus: how they work and what your venue gains

Prices change, dishes sell out and the daily special is different every day. A printed menu, though, stays the same until you print it again. A digital QR menu fixes exactly that. SmartMenuKJ started in Kardzhali with QR menus for cafés and restaurants, so here is a plain explanation of how they work and what a venue gets out of them.

## What a QR menu is
A QR menu is your menu published as a page that opens on a phone. There is a QR code on the table; the guest scans it with the phone camera and the menu opens straight away.

Guests don't need to download an app. The menu works on any phone, and you can edit it whenever you like.

## How it works, step by step
1. **The guest sits down and scans the code** on the table.
2. **The menu opens on their phone**, sorted into categories, with prices.
3. **You update it live.** Change a price or add a dish and guests see it immediately.
4. **When something runs out**, you hide it with the "sold out" switch, so nobody orders a dish you no longer have.

## What the venue gains

### No reprinting for every change
With paper, every new price or dish means a reprint or a correction by hand. With a QR menu the change takes seconds and reaches every table at once.

### A menu that is always up to date
Guests see exactly what you serve today. Daily offers and seasonal items go up when you have them and come down when they're gone.

### Several languages
A QR menu can be multilingual. Guests who don't read Bulgarian understand what they're ordering, and staff don't have to explain every dish.

### Stats on popular dishes
You can see which dishes guests look at, which makes it easier to decide what to feature and what to change.

### Easy for guests
No app, no sign-up. A phone and its camera are enough.

## Paper or QR?
A QR menu doesn't rule out a printed one; many venues keep both. It pays off most where the menu changes often: a café with daily specials, a restaurant with a seasonal menu, a place with guests who speak different languages.

## What to check before you choose
- **Can you change prices and dishes yourself**, without waiting for someone else?
- **Is there a "sold out" switch** and room for daily offers?
- **Does it open quickly on a phone**, without an app?
- **Can the menu be in several languages?**
- **Is there someone to help** after launch?

The SmartMenuKJ QR menu covers all of these, and support after launch is part of the job, not an extra.
