# QR menus for restaurants and cafés in Kardzhali

Digital QR menus are where SmartMenuKJ started. Our founder, Krasimir Uzun, began by turning the menus of nearby cafés in Kardzhali into QR codes. Today it is one of six services we offer, and still the heart of what we do.

## How it works
Guests scan the QR code on the table and the menu opens straight away on their phone, with no app to install. You stay in control: change a price, hide a sold-out dish or add a daily special in seconds, without printing a new menu.

## What you get
- **Instant QR menu** that works on any smartphone.
- **Live editing of prices and items**: changes show up immediately.
- **Multilingual menus** for guests who don't read Bulgarian.
- **Daily specials and a "sold out" switch.**
- **Statistics on popular dishes.**
