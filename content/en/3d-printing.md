# 3D printing in Kardzhali

From an idea, or a broken part, to a printed original. In Kardzhali we print prototypes, spare parts for things you can no longer buy, custom enclosures and small batches, in a range of materials and colours. The design consultation is free.

## What we print
- **Spare parts from a sample**: knobs, clips, brackets, caps and holders for things with no spare parts left. Measured, modelled and printed in a durable material.
- **Rapid prototypes** to test an idea.
- **Small production batches.**
- **Custom enclosures** and **functional and decorative prints.**
- **Gifts, figurines and keepsakes**, plus **hobby and model parts.**
- **Holders, hooks and organisers.**

## Materials and technology
- **PLA, PETG, ABS and TPU**, chosen for what the part has to do.
- **3D scanning and reverse engineering**: we turn an existing part into an accurate model for printing.

## From our work
Spare parts on demand: we reproduced a discontinued bracket and printed a full batch. See [more projects](/portfolio.html).
